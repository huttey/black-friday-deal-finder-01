A Black Friday deal finder that only lists deals a real person checked. 30 deals
from Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Costco and Nintendo, each with its sale
price, its usual price, its discount, a link to the store, the source that
reported the price, and the date it was checked (27 November 2026).

Every deal carries an honest verdict — NEW LOW, STRONG PRICE, ROUTINE SALE or
INFLATED — with the evidence behind it. Two listings on the page are called out
as not-deals: prices that were cheaper at Prime Day or last November.

Search, filter by category, store, discount and verdict, and sort by biggest
discount, lowest price or newest low. Same product at several stores is shown
together, cheapest first. The site is static files under `app/` — no build step,
no network at run time. Open `app/index.html` in a browser.
