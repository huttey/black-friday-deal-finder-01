How this page was made

Each deal was found and its price read on 27 November 2026 from the source named on the deal card — a deals roundup that names the store, or the store's own Black Friday release. The "usual" price is the one the store or source gives. The verdict is the maker's honest call: NEW LOW means the lowest price ever recorded for it, STRONG PRICE a genuinely good cut, ROUTINE SALE a small or frequent saving, and NOT A DEAL a price that was cheaper before (the evidence says when). Prices can change; check the store link before you buy.
